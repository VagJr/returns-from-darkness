## Short description

An independent, noncommercial Diablo 1 fan project rebuilding the classic online gathering place, with a community lobby, an HD visual direction and new content in development.

## Description

Some places never stop calling: Tristram, the cathedral door, and the feeling of descending one more floor with friends.

Returns from Darkness aims to bring back the social side of classic Diablo 1: meeting in a lobby, chatting, finding a party and creating a game. The planned structure is a shared community lobby with separate games for up to four players, rather than a single persistent MMO world.

The local 0.1.6 foundation integrates login, channels, chat and rooms into a customized client around DevilutionX and PvPGN. Graphical entry into Tristram was confirmed in earlier local versions; the new client flow is still being validated. Next steps include two graphical clients playing together, late joining, host departure and secure external connection tests.

The full HD artwork and interface rework, new areas and arena are still in development. The game server is not public, there is no public game download, and no release date has been announced. The public website and Discord are open for following progress and discussing the project.

Players will need files from their own Diablo installation. We do not distribute DIABDAT.MPQ or commercial game data. This is an independent, free, noncommercial fan project with no Blizzard affiliation. Diablo and Battle.net belong to their respective owners. Credit to the DevilutionX contributors and the original Diablo creators.

AI assistance is used in development, translations and concept illustrations. Concept art on the website is not gameplay footage. Any media uploaded to a project listing must be labeled accordingly; use actual captured gameplay when a field specifically requests gameplay screenshots.
